Briefing

Find the keys. Find the door. Don't be found.

  • 🔦 Your torch reveals only the tiles near you — the rest stays hidden.

  • 🚪 Escaping with enough keys awards +100 pts plus +5 pts per second remaining.
  • ⏱ You have exactly 60 seconds. The clock starts the moment you press Start below and stops the game automatically at 0.
